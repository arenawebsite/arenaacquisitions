How regular people buy a business and leave their W-2.

What the Show Covers

The real path from paycheck to owner.

Not a get-rich-quick pitch. Inside the Arena walks through what it actually takes to find, finance, buy and run an established small business, including the parts that go wrong.

01
Buying instead of starting

Why acquiring a business with existing customers, staff and cash flow can be a more realistic exit from a W-2 than building from zero, and who it is not right for.

02
Financing in plain English

How SBA 7(a) loans work from the borrower's seat: down payments, underwriting, what lenders look for, and how a business and its real estate can fit in one deal.

03
The deal, start to finish

Finding blue-collar businesses for sale, valuing them, reading the books, making an offer, due diligence, closing, and the first months as the new owner.
